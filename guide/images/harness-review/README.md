# Harness, review and supervision infographics

Three editorial diagrams in English and French, generated with Google Gemini and reviewed for text, arrows and evidence limits. They summarize proposed practices; they do not report a deployed control or a human intervention.

| Workable human oversight | [English infographic](human-oversight-en-gemini.webp) | [Infographie française](human-oversight-fr-gemini.webp) |

Presence, activation, execution and verified effect are separate observations. None establishes the others. Authorized acceptance also needs a favorable policy. The four cards are a summary, not an exhaustive client capability checklist.

The acceptance diagram requires valid evidence and an authorizing policy. A missing or unknown result pauses or stops the process. Budgets remain bounded, state persists, and a new revision requires fresh evidence. The [local control exercise](../../../examples/workflows/review-control-demo.py) uses a simulated destination; it does not establish a live GitHub merge policy.

The oversight diagram proposes conditions to assess with actual participants. Accuracy, recovery, effort and satisfaction remain separate observations. See the [comprehension exercise](../../../examples/learning-project/review-comprehension-exercise.md).

The six current assets are 1376 × 774 WebP files in a cream graph-paper style. Conversion adds three pixels above and below the generated composition to obtain 16:9 without cropping or upscaling. Articles provide equivalent prose and links to the full-size images for narrow screens. Earlier dark-background assets remain available under their original filenames.
